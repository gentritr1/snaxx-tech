# Offday

## Register
product

## Users
Employees use assigned work emails to sign in and request time off. HR and managers manage employees and approve requests within their organization.

## Product Purpose
A sellable internal PTO tool: clear team availability, employee profiles, and a reliable request and approval workflow.

## Design Principles
- Put the team calendar first.
- Clearly distinguish pending requests from approved leave.
- Keep employee and manager permissions explicit.
- Keep each organization's data isolated.

## Design assumptions
Calm, approachable, and precise. A light workspace for managers checking availability on office monitors in daylight. Restrained crimson branding, readable contrast, keyboard access, and reduced-motion support. These visual choices are implementation defaults rather than user-specified branding.
